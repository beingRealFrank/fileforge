Convert a TCX file to GPX, without uploading it

An app wants GPX and your device gave you TCX. TCX is the XML format used by Garmin Connect exports and many training apps. You can convert it to GPX on your own computer, and join several TCX files into one GPX at the same time.

Verified sample The sample below was produced by running this site's converter on a small synthetic TCX file.

Steps

  1. Get the .tcx file onto your computer. Export it from the app that recorded it, or copy it from the device. Keep the original; work on a copy.
  2. Open the GPX, FIT and TCX tool and drop the .tcx file on it. The file is read in your browser and is not uploaded.
  3. Read the point counts. The page shows points in and points out. For a single file they should match; for several files, exact duplicate points are removed and counted.
  4. Download the GPX. The GPX keeps latitude, longitude, time, elevation (if the TCX had it) and heart rate. Upload it to the app that asked for GPX.
  5. Several TCX files from one ride? Drop them all in: they are joined in time order into one track, with each file kept as its own segment if you tick that option.

What GPX does not keep

  • Power, cadence, lap summaries and calories from the TCX are not carried into the GPX; GPX has no standard place for most of them here. If you need them, keep the original TCX.
  • TCX courses and activities without GPS positions give no points, and the page says so.

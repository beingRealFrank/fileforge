Turn debit and credit columns into one signed amount column

Many statements print money out and money in in two columns (Withdrawals and Deposits, Paid out and Paid in, Debit and Credit). Spreadsheets and import tools usually want one Amount column where money out is negative. Here is how to get that from the PDF, and how to check it is right.

Verified sample The sample below was produced by running this site's converter on a synthetic statement with Paid out and Paid in columns. It is not real data.

Steps

  1. Download the statement PDF from your bank. It needs a text layer; a photo or scan will not work.
  2. Open the bank statement converter and drop the PDF on it. Nothing is uploaded.
  3. Check the columns it found. The page lists them, for example "date, description, debit, credit, balance". It recognises common names such as Withdrawals, Deposits, Paid out, Paid in, Debit and Credit.
  4. Read the balance check. It confirms that opening balance plus each amount equals the next balance. When every row reconciles, the signs and amounts are right.
  5. Download Excel or CSV. The Amount column is one signed number per row: negative for money out, positive for money in. A SUM of the column gives net movement.

When to double-check

  • If a column heading is unusual, the page says which columns it used; compare a few rows with the PDF.
  • Credit-card statements print purchases as positive amounts; use the credit card option there.
  • Scanned statements are not supported.

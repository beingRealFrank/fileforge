Bank statement PDF → Excel or CSV

Drop one statement or twelve. You get one clean list: date, description, amount (money out is negative) and balance. Everything runs on your own computer, so your statements are never uploaded.

What it does

  1. Your browser reads the text layer of each statement with pdf.js, served from this site.
  2. It finds the table, works out which columns are date, description, money out, money in, amount and balance, and ignores addresses, summaries and legal text.
  3. It reads dates in any common format, fills in a missing year from the statement period, and turns every amount into a real number.
  4. It adds up the running balance row by row and tells you how many rows reconcile. A mismatch means a row was misread, so look at it before you import.

Layouts it handles

  • Separate withdrawals and deposits columns, or paid out / paid in, or debit / credit
  • One signed amount column, with or without a balance column
  • Credit card statements with a transaction date and a post date
  • Dates such as 01/15, 15 Jan 2026, 2026-01-15 and 15.01.2026, and amounts such as 1,234.56, (12.00), 12.00- and 1.234,56
  • Descriptions that wrap onto a second line, and headers repeated on every page

Limits

  • Scanned statements (a photo or image of a page) have no text layer. They are detected and reported; OCR is not supported.
  • Every bank lays out statements differently. This tool is tested on synthetic statements in six common layouts, not on every bank. Check the balance check and the preview before you rely on the result.
  • A note printed directly under a transaction, with no gap, looks like a wrapped second line and is joined to that description. Check descriptions that look too long.
  • It does not connect to your bank, and it does not categorise transactions.

Privacy

A bank statement is about as private as a file gets. The page loads no third-party scripts, and its security policy blocks connections to other sites, so the PDF cannot be sent anywhere. The file stays in your browser's memory and is gone when you close the tab. Details, and how to check it yourself.
